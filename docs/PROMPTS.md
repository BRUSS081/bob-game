# Fase 1 — Prompt concept art

Prompt pronti da incollare in un generatore di immagini (Gemini, ChatGPT, ...). Ognuno contiene già lo STYLE in coda e il formato 16:9.
Genera **2 varianti** per prompt e caricale in `/reference` con questi nomi:

## 1. Key art

```
Landscape image, 16:9 aspect ratio. Key art: Wide side-view scene of an open tropical sea for water sports, no land: light turquoise sea with layered rolling waves and white foam in the foreground, open horizon line, puffy clouds, midday sun. A surfer rides a wave, a kitesurfer jumps high with the kite in the sky, a wing foiler glides above the water holding an inflatable wing, standing on a foil board whose hydrofoil mast points straight down from the underside of the board into the water. A few sea obstacles: a navigation buoy, a small rock, a flying seagull. Clearly separated parallax layers: sky, clouds, far sea at the horizon, mid sea, foreground water. No land, no coast, no beach. STYLE: modern high-detail pixel art for a 2D side-scrolling mobile game, crisp hard-edged pixels on a consistent grid, no anti-aliasing, no blur, limited cohesive palette of about 32 colors, soft atmospheric lighting with rim light, stepped color bands instead of smooth gradients, light turquoise sky-blue sea, clean readable silhouettes, side view, no text, no logos, no UI.
```
